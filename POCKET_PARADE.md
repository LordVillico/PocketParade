# Pocket Parade Studio

## Mission

Pocket Parade creates original collectible artwork that can be produced as buttons, magnets, stickers, keychains, and future products.

Artwork is designed to be collectible, recognizable, and fun.

---

## Design Philosophy

Small art.
Big personality.

---

## Rules

• Every illustration must be original.
• Simple enough to recognize from six feet away.
• Thick outlines.
• Minimal shading.
• Limited colors.
• White or light backgrounds.
• Works at 58mm.

---
